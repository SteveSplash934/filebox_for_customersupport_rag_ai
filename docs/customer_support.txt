Customer Support Note — Account Verification

Customers who cannot verify their email should first check their spam or junk folder.
If the verification email has not arrived after ten minutes, ask the customer to request a new message.
A verification link can only be used once and expires after 24 hours.
For account security, support agents must never ask customers to share their passwords.

Escalation: If the customer no longer has access to their registered email, route the case to Account Security.
